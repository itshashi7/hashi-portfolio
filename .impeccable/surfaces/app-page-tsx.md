## Scope
Single-page portfolio (app/page.tsx and its section components). Visitor mode: Experience — the work leads from the first viewport.

## Audience and job
Recruiters and hiring managers at product companies in Dubai, Saudi Arabia and remote teams, screening a backend engineer in a minute or two. Action: email (mailto) or download the CV.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Prove backend thinking with small, honest, working systems (a request trace, cache, breaker, cloud lanes) instead of the dev-portfolio default of avatar, gradient hero and skill bars.
OWN-WORLD: Production-dashboard calm. Cool near-black ladder #0b0c0f to #23252a with hairline borders; one amber signal #faaa49 only on live, focused or selected state; Mona Sans slightly condensed headings; JetBrains Mono for every measured value; pill controls; 16px cards.
STORY: The recruiter sees role, stack and a live request trace at once, scans case studies and widgets for depth, confirms remote/relocation and a 90-day notice in the chat, then emails or downloads the CV.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left seven columns hold the rotating H1 at display size with "See my work" and "Download CV" below; right five columns hold three intro rows; a full-width request-trace strip with an amber packet runs along the bottom; the nav pill with "Hire me" sits top centre.
FORM: Brief-pinned direction (poulo.site structure and motion, DESIGN.md system); concept-seed not run. seed: none (unavailable)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Memorable moment
The amber packet tracing a request through gateway, service, queue and cache in the hero, echoed by the gallery's request-lifecycle widget.
